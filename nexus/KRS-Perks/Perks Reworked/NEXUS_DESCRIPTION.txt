Short Description
Restores Riposte and adds new perks across combat and character skill trees.

Description
Perks Reworked packages the krs_perks module as a standalone Nexus upload. It makes Riposte and the hidden Master Strike perk visible and adds 56 perks across several skill trees, with names, descriptions, icons, and translations.

Installation instructions
Install Perks-Reworked-1.0.0.zip with Vortex, or extract krs_perks into KingdomComeDeliverance/Mods and add krs_perks to Mods/mod_order.txt. Do not enable Restore Riposte at the same time unless you intentionally choose a load order; both alter the same perk rows.

Main features
- Riposte becomes visible and unlocks at level 10 after Perfect Block.
- The hidden Master Strike perk becomes visible in Defence; Captain Bernard's taught Master Strike is not changed.
- Adds 56 perks across Defence, Sword, Axe, Mace, Unarmed, Bow, Long Weapon, Fencing, and the Player tab.
- Includes text for all 14 game languages, with English used where an upstream translation was unavailable.

Requirements
Kingdom Come: Deliverance 1.9.6, 1.9.7, or 1.9.8. No other mod is required. Perk behaviour and visibility after loading a save require a final game check before publication.
